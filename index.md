Network Engineering 101 is a online, self-paced course designed for University students who have taken a Computer Networks semester course, and want to venture into Network Engineering. The course provides direct learning material, labs, and ample further reading to take motivated students down a structured path in a Network Engineering career. Both technical and business aspects are covered.
